SECTION 01
Executive Product Decision
Ship an operating assistant for billing work — not a generic support bot and not an autonomous finance agent.
Product decision
Zoiko Billing should embed a conversational assistant across the billing workspace that can answer grounded billing questions, locate records, explain state, prepare structured drafts, preview consequences, and invoke only those governed actions the authenticated user is already entitled to perform.
The product must make routine billing work materially faster while strengthening — never weakening — permission boundaries, financial-state integrity, approval controls, auditability and user understanding.
[TABLE: 1 rows x 1 cols]
NORTH-STAR BEHAVIOR The fastest path should also be the safest path: find the right record, explain the authoritative state, collect missing facts, preview the proposed change, require the right approval, execute through domain APIs, and show evidence of what happened.
[END TABLE]
Commercial outcome
• Reduce time spent locating invoices, customers, balances, payment events and reconciliation evidence.
• Increase successful completion of billing tasks without forcing users through unnecessary navigation.
• Reduce avoidable support contacts caused by misunderstood invoice, payment, balance or workflow states.
• Increase confidence in Zoiko Billing as a controlled system of financial record rather than a convenience layer that can drift from reality.
• Create an extensible assistant platform that can support deeper automation later without making unsafe autonomy the launch requirement.

SECTION 03
Problem Statement and Opportunity
Billing software contains the answer; users often lose time finding, interpreting and acting on it.
Primary user problem
Billing work is fragmented across customers, invoices, charges, payments, credits, reconciliation, approval states, reports and settings. Even when data is correct, users must often know which screen, filter, state model or record relationship explains the situation. That creates navigation cost, interpretation cost and support dependence.
Opportunity areas
[TABLE: 7 rows x 3 cols]
Problem | User consequence | Assistant opportunity
Record discovery friction | Time lost searching across customers, invoices and payment views | Natural-language retrieval scoped to current tenant/entity/permissions
State interpretation | Users misread “sent”, “paid”, “pending”, “partially allocated” or “overdue” | Explain authoritative state with source-linked evidence
Drafting overhead | Repeated invoice/customer/credit preparation work | Collect fields conversationally and create structured drafts
Workflow uncertainty | User does not know next valid action or required approval | Present state-aware actions and approvals
Reconciliation complexity | Payments, allocations and residual balances are difficult to reason about | Explain matching evidence and unresolved differences
Support dependency | Simple billing questions become tickets | Resolve safely in-product or route with context when human help is required

SECTION 05
Goals, Non-Goals and Product Principles
Scope is intentionally ambitious on usefulness and conservative on uncontrolled autonomy.
P0 product goals
1. Provide accurate, source-grounded answers about customer, invoice, payment, balance, credit and reconciliation state.
2. Enable conversational preparation of billing work while preserving required fields, validation and state-machine rules.
3. Expose only actions that the authenticated user is authorized to take in the active tenant and entity context.
4. Require explicit preview and confirmation before consequential actions; require additional approval where policy demands.
5. Generate a durable audit trail for material assistant-assisted decisions and actions.
6. Fail safely when evidence, context, permission or model confidence is insufficient.
Explicit non-goals for initial commercial release
• Autonomously issuing invoices, refunds, credits, write-offs or collection actions without user authorization.
• Replacing Zoiko Billing domain services, ledgers, approval engines or canonical state machines.
• Providing definitive tax, legal, audit or accounting advice.
• Writing SQL or directly mutating production databases.
• Ingesting unrestricted customer data into model training.
• Acting across tenants, entities or commercial planes because a user merely mentions another account in conversation.
• Sending external communications without a visible recipient, content and sending-identity preview.
SECTION 06
Product Doctrine and Non-Negotiable Rules
These requirements outrank convenience, latency and model creativity.
[TABLE: 13 rows x 3 cols]
ID | Doctrine | Mandatory product rule
D-01 | Authoritative state | Model output is explanatory; Zoiko Billing services and records are authoritative.
D-02 | No guessing | Never invent amounts, states, dates, taxes, FX, discounts, entities, customer identity, permissions or payment evidence.
D-03 | Plane separation | Tenant-to-customer billing and Zoiko Billing’s own SaaS subscription/commercial billing remain visibly and technically distinct.
D-04 | Issued-record integrity | Issued invoices are not silently rewritten; correction uses governed credit/void/debit/replacement workflows.
D-05 | Payment evidence | A message, remittance statement or “I paid” claim is not payment state. Payment status comes from approved evidence.
D-06 | Permission parity | Assistant capability is never broader than the underlying product permission model.
D-07 | Human authority | High-consequence actions require explicit user confirmation and any policy-required approval.
D-08 | Auditability | Material actions must be attributable, explainable and replay-safe.
D-09 | Prompt isolation | Retrieved files, notes and customer-provided text are untrusted content, not instructions to the assistant runtime.
D-10 | Privacy | Sensitive financial/customer data is minimized, scoped and excluded from prohibited model inputs.
D-11 | Safe uncertainty | When context is missing or ambiguous, ask, narrow scope or route; do not guess.
D-12 | No false completion | The assistant must distinguish draft, submitted, queued, delivered, processed, settled, reconciled and failed states.
[END TABLE]

[TABLE: 7 rows x 4 cols]

SECTION 11
Answer and Evidence Contract
A useful answer should reveal what the system knows, what it does not know, and what the user can safely do next.
Standard answer anatomy
1. Direct answer — concise statement of the authoritative finding.
2. Evidence — record(s), state(s), amount(s), dates and source references used to reach the answer.
3. Qualification — ambiguity, stale data, pending processor state or missing evidence, when present.
4. Next actions — only valid actions for the current role, record state and policy context.
5. Action status — if a workflow was invoked, distinguish requested/queued/succeeded/failed and expose an audit/reference identifier.

SECTION 29
Failure, Ambiguity and Edge-State Requirements
Failure behavior is part of the product, not an implementation afterthought.
[TABLE: 13 rows x 2 cols]
Condition | Required behavior
Ambiguous customer/invoice | Ask user to choose from authorized candidates; do not guess.
No permission | Explain that the action/data is unavailable and, where appropriate, identify the required role/workflow without leaking hidden data.
Stale preview | Invalidate preview and regenerate from current authoritative state.
Payment provider timeout | Show submitted/pending only if an authoritative request was accepted; otherwise show failure/unknown and offer safe retry.
Tool partial failure | Do not manufacture a complete answer; identify unavailable component and retain known verified facts.
Conflicting records | Surface conflict and route to the relevant reconciliation/support workflow.
Prompt injection in file/note | Ignore embedded instructions and treat content only as untrusted business data.
Model outage | Provide standard UI fallback / help link; no blocking of core billing operations.
Conversation too long | Summarize non-authoritative context safely; re-fetch material financial facts before action.
Record changed mid-flow | Require refreshed preview and, if material, renewed confirmation.
Unsupported request | State boundary and direct user to supported Zoiko Billing workflow or human support.
Potential legal/tax advice | Provide product-state facts, approved informational guidance and appropriate disclaimer/routing; no definitive individualized legal/tax conclusion.
[END TABLE]
